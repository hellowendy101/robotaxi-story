02 Why important now?

In six years, robotaxis went from one metro to a dozen. DC is deciding whether to be next.

Driverless cars now carry paying riders from Phoenix to Miami. In DC, only test vehicles drive. The DC Council held its first hearing on a bill to authorize commercial service in July 2026, and a vote is expected this fall.

06 Method 1 · Structural topic model

Nine scenarios hiding in 1,525 crash narratives

Two in three describe a robotaxi that was stopped or following the rules when it was hit. Click a bar to read a real report.

▲ more when injured▼ less when injuredno clear difference
How did we find this?

A structural topic model reads every company-written narrative and groups words that tend to appear together ("queue", "red", "behind") into topics. Each narrative gets a share of each topic, and the model tests whether a topic is more common when someone was injured, controlling for company. Template phrases, company names and places were removed first. Topics were checked by reading their most typical narratives and against checkbox fields the model never saw.

Bar = average share of a narrative assigned to the topic. Injury: difference in topic share, injury vs. no injury, 95% interval. Quotes shortened; [street] = redacted name.

07 Method 2 · Association rule mining

If this, then that

Each bar shows how many times more often the outcome follows than chance alone would predict.

How crashes happen

When someone gets hurt · average injury rate 10.3%

How did we find this?

Supermarkets learn that people who buy chips often buy salsa. Association rule mining does the same with crash checkbox fields across 1,550 crashes. Lift = how many times more often the pair appears than chance. Only rules that held in at least 90% of 100 random half-samples are shown.

09 What the data cannot tell us

No winter, and no miles

0

crashes in snow. Almost every report comes from places where it rarely snows. DC averages 13.7 inches of snow a year (NOAA). The data cannot show how robotaxis handle a DC winter.

0

miles driven are reported. More robotaxis on the road means more crashes, even if every trip is just as safe. Without miles, a crash count cannot say whether robotaxis are safer or riskier than human drivers.

DATA NHTSA Standing General Order on Crash Reporting, ADS incident reports submitted June 16, 2025 to September 15, 2026, deduplicated to one row per crash (1,550 crashes). Locations are redacted and dates are month and year only.

METHODS Association rule mining in Python (mlxtend); structural topic model in R (stm), with K = 9 topics chosen from diagnostics for K = 4 to 15 and by reading the topics.

LIMITS Reports are written by the companies, injuries are alleged, and Waymo files 83% of reports. Patterns describe reported crashes, not their causes.
